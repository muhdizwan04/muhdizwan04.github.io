The Journey

SwiftApp Dev with Swift — Associate
Ai / PsAdobe Specialist

I'm a well-organised computer science student at UiTM. I finished a Diploma in Computer Science there and I'm now reading for CS259 — Bachelor of Information Systems (Hons.) Intelligent Systems Engineering at UiTM Merbok. I work at the intersection of logical programming and aesthetic design — the systems have to run, and they have to look like someone cared.

I don't just write code; I build businesses. I've run a sublimation jersey printing company since 2021 — drawing every kit in Illustrator and Photoshop, then carrying it through to finished, printed product. What started as one-off commissions now dresses universities, schools and corporate teams across Malaysia. It taught me to read a brief, hold a deadline, and answer to a client who can see exactly what they paid for.

AIZZTECH is where that same discipline becomes software. I co-founded it with Muhammad Syahrul Izwan to build custom platforms, cloud infrastructure and AI-driven systems for clients — and to ship products of our own: Rencana, a student planner, and Belanje, a personal and business finance app built for Malaysia.
